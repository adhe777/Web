About Us

MEA Engineering College (MEAEC) is a premier private self-financing engineering institution established in 2002, located at Nellikunnu near Perinthalmanna, Malappuram district, Kerala. Managed by the Muslim Educational Association (MEA) and sponsored by the "Samastha Kerala Jammiyyathul Ulama", the college holds the distinction of being the first NAAC-accredited self-financing engineering college in the district.

Course Offered

The college offers diverse, KTU-affiliated programs spanning 4-year B.Tech degrees (in core engineering, AI, and IT fields), 4-year BCA (Hons), 2-year MCA and M.Tech postgraduate options, and advanced doctoral Ph.D. research tracks.
